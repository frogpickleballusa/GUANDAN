Rules used here
  • 4, 6 or 8 players with one deck per two players, so everyone gets 27 cards. Two teams sit alternately: odd seats are Jade, even seats are Amber. Play goes to the right.
  • Each team's starting level is set before the deal (normally 2), along with which team's level the first hand uses. After that, the hand's level is the level of the team that went out first last hand. Level cards rank above A and below the jokers; the red-heart level cards (gold edge, one per deck) are wild and stand in for any card except a joker.
  • Plays: single, pair, triple, full house (triple + pair), straight of 5, tube (3 straight pairs), plate (2 straight triples). A can be high or low in a run.
  • Bombs beat everything above: 4 of a kind < 5 of a kind < straight flush < 6 of a kind … < all the jokers together (4, 6 or 8 of them).
  • Your hand arrives sorted. Select cards and tap Group to keep them together (a bomb or straight you are saving); tapping a grouped card selects the whole group, and Ungroup breaks it up. Groups are only on your device.
  • Take-back: the player who just moved can ask to undo that one move. The table pauses until the host (whoever created the table) approves or denies it. The host's own take-backs need no approval.
  • Pre-pass: tap Pass before your turn and you pass automatically the moment your turn comes (once). If the table has been cleared and you would lead, it cancels instead. When it isn't your turn, Play and Pass become Pre-play and Pre-pass. Pre-play: select cards and tap it; they are played the moment your turn comes if they beat the table, otherwise it cancels and leaves them selected. Other players' card counts show as 10+ until they are down to 10. Last plays shows the most recent plays of the hand, newest first.
  • Turn timer: set in game setup (Off, or 10 to 60 seconds, default 20). It applies to every turn except the very first turn of the game. If time runs out you pass; if you were leading a new trick, the lead moves to the next player.
  • Auto-pass: if it's your turn, you have fewer than 4 cards, and the play on the table uses more cards than you hold, you pass automatically. Only the number of cards counts, never their values.
  • When you go out and nobody beats your last play, your next teammate still holding cards leads.
  • The hand ends once only one team still has cards. Add up each team's finishing places (1st = 1, 2nd = 2 …); the lower total wins the hand, and a tie goes to the team that went out first. Taking all the top places moves up 3; the rest scale down to +2 or +1. With 4 players this is the usual 1-2 = +3, 1-3 = +2, 1-4 = +1. With 6 players: total 6 = +3, 7–8 = +2, 9–10 = +1.
  • Tribute (done for you): on a +3 every loser pays; otherwise the losing team's last finisher pays (on a +2 with 6 or 8 players, the last two pay). Each payer's best non-wild card goes to a winner (the best card to the winner who finished highest). If that highest card comes in more than one suit, the payer picks which suit to give. Each winner then picks a card of 10 or lower to give back, and play starts once every card has been returned. No tribute if the payers hold every big joker. The best payer leads.
  • Win: take a hand while playing your own team's A level.

Each phone only ever receives its own cards, and the server checks every move.
